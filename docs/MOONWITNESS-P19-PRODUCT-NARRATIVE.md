
# MOONWITNESS — PRODUCT NARRATIVE: WITNESS → MESSAGE

**Status:** Narrative specification for P18/P19 · 2026-10-25. Additive to [Product Story v1](./PRODUCT-STORY.md), not a silent replacement.
**Related:** [Master Grandplan](./MOONWITNESS-SRGBL-V8-GRANDPLAN.md) · [Experience Blueprint](./MOONWITNESS-SRGBL-V8-BLUEPRINT.md) · [Local AI Handoff](./MOONWITNESS-LOCAL-AI-HANDOFF.md).

> **WHEN THE MOON WITNESS, WE SHARE A MESSAGE.**

**TRACE THE TEXT. EXAMINE THE WITNESS. PRESERVE THE DIFFERENCE.**

## 1. The story we must preserve

A reader finds a fragment of revelation or scripture. Without the edition, source, neighboring verses, genre, speaker, recipient or rights, the quote sounds like a universal verdict. A second reader offers a comforting interpretation without provenance; that also loses the path from source to claim.

MoonWitness is an observatory that restores **the source-to-claim path**. It does not choose one doctrine, judgment or comforting sentiment for every passage. **TEXT ≠ CLAIM ≠ INTERPRETATION ≠ FRAMEWORK ≠ JUDGMENT.** The original Product Story's journey is retained: **Discover → Trace → Read Context → Examine Key Witness → Cite**.

SRGBL v8 adds an optional research experience to that journey; it is not a replacement for a source-first public reader.

## 2. Text → Witness → Message

**TEXT:** Which original source, passage, edition, script, translation, artifact and rights?  
**WITNESS:** What precise textual observation/key/relation is evidenced here?  
**MESSAGE:** Under which audience, method, frame and limitations can a reviewer derive a responsible interpretation?

The result may be UNKNOWN, disputed, or incomplete. One verse may yield many candidate keys, differently framed messages and non-identical translations. No output replaces the original text or becomes a definitive tafsir without attributed source/review.

## 3. Five-part investigative narrative

| Layer | Reader's question | Source-bound outcome |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Source & Sense | Where did this exact witness come from? | Original/edition/artifact/provenance/rights and acquisition limits |
| R2 Reality | What does the source explicitly say? | Observed textual content and genre/context, not historical proof |
| G3 Governing Order | What relations or conditions are supportable? | Linked claims, hypotheses, alternatives and counterevidence |
| B4 Being | Who speaks, is addressed or acts; in which frame? | Speaker/recipient/genre/time/agency and attributed interpretation |
| L5 Light | What bounded direction/message can be responsibly expressed? | Derived synthesis, uncertainty, boundary note and next evidence |

**Light is an editorial metaphor for orientation, not a physical measurement or proof of divine action.** Do not promote later stages past unsupported prerequisites. A draft L5 idea may exist while S1 is pending, but earns no cumulative supported maturity.

## 4. Key Witness: the missing middle

PASSAGE + ORIGINAL TEXTUAL WITNESS → FRAGMENT → KEY → RELATION → FRAME → DIRECTION → EVIDENCE → REVIEW → DERIVED MESSAGE + BOUNDARY.

A key is a controlled semantic **pointer**, not final divine meaning. Frame includes agent, recipient, domain, scope and genre. Direction −2..+2 belongs to a framed key bundle, not the worth of a verse. Disagreement is stored rather than hidden. Exploratory tokens like faith, good deeds and paradise promise must not silently enter the unratified P18 controlled registry.

A severe warning must not be erased to make it sound “positive”; source accuracy precedes editorial preference.

## 5. Swing Swing Key and Unlock Treasure

**SWING THE KEY. DISCOVER THE TREASURES OF THE QURAN.**

**ROTATE THE RELATIONSHIP, NOT THE TRUTH.**

Swing makes questions/candidates by rotation of direction, polarity, subject/object, cause/effect, perspective, temporal position and semantic opposition. Max three hops. Every transformed candidate starts UNVERIFIED, never inherits scores, must trace independent source support and possible contradiction, and can be declined by reviewers.

Unlock Treasure means **a traceable and reviewed new relation**, not a hidden numerical code, supernatural fact detector or new revelation.

## 6. Positive Correction / miracle claims

Neutral comparison preserves parallels and doctrinal differences. Quran-anchored interpretation is a **separately chosen Islamic theological reading** (for example, interpretation of QS 5:48), with explicit Quranic passage, other scriptural passage, source edition, exegesis, frame and rebuttals. It may mark CORRECTS_WITHIN_FRAME, not assert neutral universal proof. Hebrew/Greek/other source text is never silently rewritten by a standardized gloss.

A Positive Miracle is a **source-attributed theological claim**. An algorithm, checksum or score cannot prove a miracle.

## 7. OFFSIDE → ONSIDE

OFFSIDE is an editorial **possible context mismatch**: speaker, actor, recipient, source, scope, genre, timeframe or evidence. ONSIDE is a candidate repair, requiring sourced justification. The ASSAFAAT-30 / Master Gula referee-to-coach story is historical creative metaphor, *not literal wording* of QS 37:30 or QS 20:5. The QS 37:30 research fixture must include neighboring context.

## 8. The feel of the product

Preserve **wonder without deception**: LINES + LIGHT + MOTION + ELECTRICITY, restrained and accessible. Lines connect *real references*; light highlights source locations; movement guides browsing; none implies paranormal proof. RTL Arabic/Hebrew and reduced-motion accessibility are mandatory. Public screens should not look like a scripture-leaderboard.

Copy patterns:
- “Cari sumbernya lebih dulu.”
- “Key ini masih kandidat; bukti dan batasnya di sini.”
- “R2 terdukung; G3 belum diverifikasi.”
- “Dalam pembacaan Quran-anchored; bukan vonis universal.”
- “Reviewer berbeda pendapat; perbedaannya tersimpan.”
- “Treasure candidate: hubungan baru, belum disahkan.”

Avoid “AI membuktikan mukjizat”, “semua kitab identik”, “ayat ini pasti negatif”, “UNKNOWN = 50”, “interpretasi ini adalah teks asli.”

## 9. Ownership of the story

Corpus owns TEXT/source/rights/provenance and derived semantic **research records**. Broader fictional characters, episodes and personal witness material have separate storytelling ownership and can only be displayed as attributed editorial material. They must not be written into canonical scripture, or reused as verified real events without evidence.

## 10. Audience-specific result

**Reader:** trace a quote, browse contextual lanes and copy an exact citation; no scoring knowledge required.

**Researcher:** choose frame, inspect S1–L5, alternatives, key bundle, bounded Swing, source-level disagreement, and missing evidence.

**Annotator:** attach one keyed assessment with source selector, evidence, method and status.

**Reviewer:** verify source/rights/context and record authenticated decision and dissent; a generated AI interpretation is not a human review.

**Local AI engineer:** take one micro issue, work only on the existing dev branch, produce test evidence and an atomic commit; not an editorial authority.
